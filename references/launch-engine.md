# Launch Engine

A launch should be a sequence, not a single announcement.

## Phase 1 — Seed

Introduce the problem, tension or change before the product reveal.

Goal: make the category/problem salient.

## Phase 2 — Tease

Show fragments:

- prototype;
- screenshot;
- backstage;
- partial result;
- waitlist;
- test;
- unexplained detail.

Goal: create curiosity.

## Phase 3 — Reveal

Explain clearly:

- what it is;
- who it is for;
- what problem it solves;
- what changed;
- how to access it.

Do not preserve ambiguity after the reveal.

## Phase 4 — Proof

Demonstrate:

- real usage;
- live workflow;
- customer proof;
- benchmark;
- comparison;
- test;
- result.

## Phase 5 — Event

Concentrate attention with:

- live session;
- launch event;
- workshop;
- webinar;
- release moment;
- community call;
- store/event activation.

The event should include interaction and proof, not just a sales monologue.

## Phase 6 — Offer

Define a legitimate reason to act now:

- launch pricing;
- limited cohort;
- early-access bonus;
- real inventory limit;
- onboarding bonus;
- bundle;
- priority;
- implementation slot.

Avoid fake scarcity.

## Phase 7 — Reaction

Capture:

- first users;
- questions;
- feedback;
- reactions;
- implementation;
- outcomes;
- objections.

## Phase 8 — Echo

Turn the event into additional assets:

- clips;
- FAQs;
- testimonials;
- emails;
- ads;
- landing-page sections;
- sales enablement;
- case studies.

## Phase 9 — Retarget

Follow up with people who:

- visited;
- registered;
- watched;
- replied;
- opened;
- abandoned checkout;
- started trial;
- attended but did not buy.

## Phase 10 — Evergreen

Convert the best launch insights into an ongoing acquisition system.

## Live commerce generalized

The mechanism is:

**synchronized attention + interaction + proof + offer + immediate action**

Translate by market.

### SaaS

- live demo;
- teardown;
- onboarding session;
- office hours;
- feature release;
- build-in-public session.

### B2B

- workshop;
- roundtable;
- benchmark review;
- diagnostic session;
- webinar with direct CTA.

### Professional services

- live analysis;
- Q&A;
- case walkthrough;
- educational session leading to consultation.

### E-commerce

- live shopping;
- drop;
- unboxing;
- demonstration.

### Local business

- opening;
- tasting;
- in-person event;
- launch;
- local creator activation.

## Event checklist

Before:

- define audience;
- define promise;
- prepare infrastructure;
- create anticipation;
- confirm proof;
- confirm fulfillment capacity.

During:

- show product;
- interact;
- answer objections;
- present one main offer;
- make CTA simple.

After:

- follow up;
- repurpose;
- gather proof;
- analyze conversion;
- keep the best pieces evergreen.
