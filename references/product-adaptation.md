# Product & Market Adaptation

Transfer mechanisms, not surface tactics.

## Consumer products

Useful mechanisms:

- entertainment;
- visual demonstration;
- UGC;
- creator distribution;
- community;
- live commerce;
- drops;
- bundles;
- repeat purchase.

Primary risk: attention without retention or fulfillment.

## SaaS

Translate tactics into:

- screen-recorded proof;
- use-case demos;
- build in public;
- feature teasers;
- live demos;
- free tools;
- activation loops;
- templates;
- referral;
- lifecycle content.

Primary metric: activation/retention, not just signup.

## B2B

Prioritize:

- founder expertise;
- customer pain specificity;
- workshops;
- benchmark data;
- cases;
- demos;
- partner distribution;
- targeted outbound;
- sales-assisted conversion.

Replace "fan community" with:

- champions;
- user groups;
- customer councils;
- peer communities;
- partner ecosystems.

## High-ticket services

Prioritize:

- credibility;
- diagnostic content;
- proof;
- authority;
- consultation;
- case detail;
- objection handling;
- personal follow-up.

Avoid discount-first positioning.

## Local business

Prioritize:

- geographic community;
- Google/Maps;
- WhatsApp;
- local creators;
- events;
- referrals;
- partnerships;
- local media;
- repeat purchase.

## Professional services

Show:

- method;
- judgment;
- before/after process;
- diagnosis;
- educational proof;
- client journey;
- deliverables.

Protect confidentiality.

## Marketplaces/platforms

Remember the two-sided problem.

Build loops for both sides:

- supply acquisition;
- demand acquisition;
- trust;
- liquidity;
- referrals;
- proof of successful matches.

## Product-led vs sales-led

If self-serve:

optimize product proof, activation and in-product referral.

If sales-led:

optimize qualified demand, sales conversations, demos, proof and follow-up.

Do not force a consumer-style launch onto an enterprise purchase cycle.

## Adaptation test

Before using a tactic, ask:

1. What psychological/commercial mechanism makes this tactic work?
2. Does that mechanism exist in this market?
3. What is the native format for this buyer?
4. What would make the tactic credible instead of gimmicky?
