# Playbooks

## Zero customers

Goal: get legitimate proof quickly.

Use:

- founder demo;
- prototype;
- sandbox;
- clearly labeled simulation;
- beta;
- tester;
- design partner;
- benchmark;
- build in public;
- walkthrough;
- problem content;
- manual outreach.

Do not fake testimonials or clients.

### 72-hour move

1. create one strong demo;
2. identify 30 target users;
3. send contextual outreach;
4. book conversations;
5. turn objections into content;
6. recruit 3–5 testers/design partners.

## Tiny audience

Goal: escape the current follower graph.

Required:

- at least two non-owned distribution channels;
- one repeatable founder-led action;
- one borrowed-audience experiment.

Possible mix:

- 20 target DMs;
- 10 high-value comments;
- 2 partner pitches;
- 1 guest post/podcast/live;
- 1 community contribution;
- 1 demo asset.

## First 100 customers

Do not optimize for scale too early.

Focus on:

- narrow ICP;
- direct conversations;
- high-touch onboarding;
- objection tracking;
- customer language;
- founder follow-up;
- referral moments;
- product learning.

The first 100 are not only revenue. They are the raw material for the scalable system.

## Strong audience, weak sales

Diagnose:

- audience-product mismatch;
- weak offer;
- poor clarity;
- insufficient proof;
- weak CTA;
- pricing friction;
- checkout friction;
- product hidden behind content;
- too much education, too little commerce.

Do not automatically seek more reach.

## Strong sales, weak retention

Pause aggressive acquisition.

Investigate:

- expectation gap;
- onboarding;
- product quality;
- support;
- delivery;
- time-to-value;
- pricing;
- fit;
- cancellation reasons.

Fix the leak before scaling the faucet.

## Low-budget launch

Use:

- founder-led content;
- targeted outreach;
- partners;
- niche communities;
- email;
- live/demo;
- customer/tester proof;
- repurposing.

Spend money only after learning which message and offer convert.

## Creator/partner strategy

Choose partners by:

- trust fit;
- audience relevance;
- credibility;
- format fit;
- conversion potential;
- willingness to genuinely use/understand product.

Do not rank only by follower count.

## Turn customers into distribution

Identify a shareable moment.

Then make it easy to:

- post;
- invite;
- refer;
- showcase;
- review;
- compare;
- teach.

Add recognition or incentive where appropriate.
