# Guardrails

## Demand vs Capacity Gate

Before creating a large demand spike, verify capacity.

### Digital product

Check:

- infrastructure;
- payments;
- onboarding;
- support;
- quotas/APIs;
- monitoring;
- incident response.

### Physical product

Check:

- real inventory;
- production;
- packaging;
- logistics;
- returns;
- customer support.

### Services

Check:

- calendar;
- team capacity;
- SLA;
- handoff;
- quality control;
- waitlist.

### B2B

Check:

- sales capacity;
- demos;
- implementation;
- customer success;
- contracts;
- support.

Rule:

> Never design a campaign whose success would predictably break the promised experience.

If capacity is constrained, use limited cohorts/lots/slots with truthful limits.

## Truth & trust

Never recommend:

- fake testimonials;
- invented customers;
- fabricated reviews;
- fake scarcity;
- fake countdowns;
- fabricated metrics;
- manipulated before/after;
- hidden material sponsorship where disclosure is required;
- deleting legitimate criticism to create a false reputation;
- selling inventory/capacity that does not exist without clear pre-sale terms;
- spam;
- dark patterns;
- astroturfing.

## Claims

Claims must match evidence.

Especially for regulated or high-stakes markets, avoid unsupported claims about:

- health;
- financial outcomes;
- legal outcomes;
- guaranteed income;
- safety;
- performance.

## Reputation recovery

When a real failure occurs:

1. stop worsening the problem;
2. acknowledge facts;
3. explain corrective action;
4. give a resolution path;
5. fix operations;
6. communicate progress;
7. use results—not spin—as proof of recovery.

## Privacy

Do not expose customer data, confidential material or private conversations without permission.

## Community transparency

If rewards, affiliates or ambassador incentives exist, disclose them appropriately.

Trust compounds when incentives are understandable.
